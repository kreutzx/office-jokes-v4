> Dana from Finance booked a 'quick sync' for 15 minutes; the calendar invite is now in its third quarter and has its own agenda. Last week it promoted itself to 'All-Hands.'

## Why this line

One character (Dana from Finance), one scene (a 'quick sync' booked for 15 minutes), and one escalation that never stops: the invite survives into its third quarter, grows its own agenda, and finally promotes itself to 'All-Hands.' Every beat is concrete — a name, a department, a 15-minute booking, a quarter count — and the punchline is the promotion, the meeting becoming the boss of the office it was meant to serve.
